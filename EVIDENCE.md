# Evidence and Competitive Review


Public evidence reviewed November 2, 2026. Vendor descriptions establish advertised capabilities, not measured effectiveness, deployed availability at every customer, or demand for this proposal. No outreach sent.

This makes it a stronger initial research candidate than the generic group catalog alone established. Exact controls, installed base, procedure ownership and support economics remain unverified.
